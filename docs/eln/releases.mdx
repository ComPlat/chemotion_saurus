The ELN follows the [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/) scheme. The Chemotion team strives to make releases on a regular basis. However, not all releases are packaged for external releases. After a round of feedback from internal users at KIT, only versions that are considered stable are released via the [chemCLI](install-configure/ChemCLI) tool.

## Latest Releases

Latest releases of the ELN, that are currently supported, are listed below.
Support means that these pages stay available for that version, and that
[chemCLI](install-configure/ChemCLI) can keep an instance updated
automatically. Security issues are always addressed in the release that comes
next, never in a past one, whatever its support date. For a version past the
date given below, please contact the
[Helpdesk](https://chemotion.net/helpdesk).

### Version 4

#### Version 4.0.0 (October 2026)

Version 4.0.0 is a major release with breaking changes.

:::danger[Read before upgrading]

Work through [Upgrading](install-configure/upgrading) first, and take a
database backup. Several of the changes are silent — nothing fails visibly, and
the symptom appears days later. Support in chemCLI follows with a later release
of the CLI; until then the published payload installs version 3.

:::

### What changed for users

Everything below is compared with **version 3.1.2**, the last version
distributed to users.

#### Collections and sharing

Version 4.0.0 reworks how collections are shared. This is a **breaking change**: the two
former mechanisms — shared collections and *synchronized* collections — are replaced by a
single sharing model, and the permission levels were renamed and reordered.

What this means in practice:

- **Every collection has exactly one owner.** Existing collections are migrated
  automatically.
- **There is one kind of share.** A share grants access to the owner's own collection, and
  the people it is shared with see that collection live, under **Shared with me**. Synchronized
  collections are gone, because every share now behaves the way they did.
- **Permission levels form a strict ladder**: *Read elements* → *Edit elements* → *Add
  elements* → *Remove elements* → *Manage shares* → *Pass ownership*. Existing shares are
  remapped automatically and keep the capabilities they had, so nothing needs to be
  re-shared. Version 3's *Write* and *Share* both become **Add elements**, and *Delete*
  and *Import Elements* both become **Remove elements**; the two new rungs, *Edit
  elements* and *Manage shares*, are never produced by the upgrade.
- **Only an element's owner can destroy it.** A collaborator with *Remove elements* unlinks
  an element from the shared collection instead of deleting it.
- **Ownership can be passed on**, as a two-step offer and acceptance: the owner offers
  ownership at the top permission level, and the recipient clicks the swap icon on the
  collection and confirms.
- **"Remove from all Collections" no longer deletes globally.** It unlinks the selection
  from the user's own collections and deletes only the elements left in no collection at all.

Version 3's *Shared by me* and *Synchronized with me* tree branches are gone. The
collections that used to appear in them are gathered under an ordinary collection named
**My projects with others** during the upgrade.

See [Collections](ui/collections) and [Sharing Collections](ui/collections/sharing.mdx) for
the full description.

#### Samples and mixtures

- **Moisture**, **particle size** and **physical state** are recorded on the
  [Properties tab](ui/details#properties-tab).
- Melting and boiling points accept hyphenated ranges and decimal commas
  alongside the space-separated ranges version 3 took, and a **Decomposed**
  checkbox covers substances that decompose before melting.
- Research plans carry a generated **short label**.
- A [mixture](ui/elements/samples/mixtures) may hold the same molecule more
  than once, and components can be moved or merged.

#### Reactions

- Amounts can be shown in **μmol**.
- A reaction has a **type**, and the new
  [Interaction type](ui/elements/reactions#reaction-types) reshapes the scheme
  for guest–host studies. A **pH** field is available for either type.
- **Concentration** can be typed in, the volume it is measured against is
  [chosen per reaction](ui/details#concentration-and-reaction-volume), and the
  reaction volume can be locked against recalculation.
- Reagent and solvent dropdowns gain a **Most Used** tab.
- [Variations](ui/details#variations-tab) gain a concentration column, can be
  filled from an analysis data file, and now link an analysis from a `-v<id>`
  file name uploaded straight into a dataset, not only one assigned from the
  inbox.

#### Chemicals and safety data sheets

- Safety phrases and pictograms can be
  [curated by hand](ui/inventory#adding-safety-phrases-manually).
- Sheets are searched across Sigma-Aldrich, Thermo Fisher and PubChem, by CAS
  number, name or product number, with up to five kept per sample.
- [Extract from sheet](ui/inventory#extract-from-sheet) reads a saved sheet for
  its H and P codes and its physical properties.
- Amounts, volumes and temperatures accept a decimal comma.

#### Interface

- [Notifications](ui#notifications) can be searched and browsed, not only the
  unread ones, and archived.
- [My labels](ui/toolbar#my-labels) adds or removes labels across a whole
  selection.
- Text templates can be kept [per user](ui/text-templates).
- The list filters moved into a [panel that folds away](ui/lists#filtering-lists).
- The [calendar](ui/calendar) gains a New Event button, filters, a per-day
  agenda and a status on each event.
- The [Explorer](ui/toolbar#explorer) draws a collection as a graph of its
  samples and reactions.
- [Cell lines](ui/elements/cell-lines) are found by a search across all
  elements, and have their own advanced-search fields.
- Ketcher is updated to 3.18.
- An analysis's [preview image](ui/details#the-preview-image) can be chosen
  rather than picked automatically.
- [LC/MS](../services/chemspectra/lcms) data open in a layout of their own,
  with a mass spectrum read off at a chosen retention time.
- Every sample import produces an [import report](ui/import#the-import-report)
  that can be corrected and imported again.

#### Wellplates

- A new [wellplate](ui/elements/wellplates) starts without a size and can be
  resized after it has been saved, as long as no filled well would be lost.
- **Wellplate import templates saved from version 3 no longer work**: a
  Molarity column was added at E, shifting the readout columns. Download a
  fresh template, or insert an empty column at E headed `Molarity`.

#### Accounts and groups

- Users can create [authentication tokens](ui/first-steps#settings-authentication-token)
  for scripts and external tools, which requires two-factor authentication.
- In a [group](admin/groups), administrators no longer have to be members, and
  a group always keeps at least one administrator.

### What changed for administrators

- The three PubChem background queues became one, so a worker started with an
  explicit queue list needs updating, and `CRON_CONFIG_PC_LCSS` is obsolete.
- Collections owned by a group account need attention — see
  [Upgrading](install-configure/upgrading).
- Safety data sheet text extraction can be handed to an optional
  [PDF text service](install-configure/configuration#safety-data-sheet-text-extraction).
- New SMTP timeouts, and an
  [in-app guide](install-configure/configuration#in-app-guide) with an
  alternate logo. Version 3.1.4 has the SMTP timeouts and the guide as well,
  but not the guide's title setting or the alternate logo.
- **Sample Explorer** is new, and **Weighing Tasks**, which every user had in
  version 3, is now an optional component. Both ship switched **off** in
  production, so an upgraded instance loses Weighing Tasks until it is enabled
  in [`config/ui_components.yml`](install-configure/configuration#ui-components).

The detailed release notes for version 4.0.0 are
[on GitHub](https://github.com/ComPlat/chemotion_ELN/releases/tag/v4.0.0).

### Version 3

**Supported until 31 October 2027.**

Upgrading to version 3 from version 2 needs a few deliberate steps — most
importantly setting `OTP_SECRET_KEY`, without which the ELN does not start at
all. See [Upgrading](install-configure/upgrading).

#### Version 3.0.0 (March 2026)

The new UI comes with several changes such as a new navigation structure, new UI adjustments (e.g. flexible change of the width), and changes to the reactions UI.

The new navigation structure includes a new panel below the summary of collections for collection management, and is drag and drop accessible via new symbols. [Inbox](ui/inbox), weighing tasks, [calendar](ui/calendar) and notifications are now reached from the top bar of the application.

The reactions UI now provides additional information in the reaction table if more space is given to the reaction panel. The button for changing the reaction scheme information (samples given with name or structure) is moved to the reaction image, and my labels for reactions are now in scheme (not properties tab).

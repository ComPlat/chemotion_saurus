A **Research Plan** <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faFileAlt} size="lg"/> is a modular, multi-segment element used to document experimental procedures, synthesis workflows, project notes, and protocols. Each plan automatically receives a non-editable short label of the form `<initials>-RP<number>` (`ABC-RP3`, for example), counting up per user and shown before the plan name in the element list.

Unlike single-purpose elements such as individual samples or reactions, a research plan provides a structured canvas combining narrative text, tables, molecular structures, reaction schemes, images, and embedded ELN elements.

## Creation

To create a new research plan:
1. Select the desired target [collection](../collections) in the collection sidebar.
2. Click the green <Btn mixed={[faPlus, "CREATE"]} color={"success"}/> button in the [toolbar](../toolbar) and choose **Create Research Plan**.
3. The new plan opens in the [details panel](../details#detail-panel-for-research-plan).

## Adding and managing content segments

Research plans are composed of modular segments that can be added and arranged to match the workflow:

### Available segment types

- **Text Editor (`Add Text Editor`)**: Insert rich text segments supporting headings, lists, chemical symbols, and personal/global [text templates](../text-templates).
- **Table Editor (`Add Table Editor`)**: Add customizable tables for experimental parameters, matrices, or measurement series. Press `Enter` to commit the active cell, then use `Tab` (`Shift+Tab`) or arrow keys to move cleanly to adjacent cells.
- **Ketcher Editor (`Add Ketcher Editor`)**: Embed an interactive [structure editor](../ketcher) segment for molecules or reaction schemes.
- **Image (`Add Image`)**: Upload images, spectra screenshots, or diagrams with an adjustable zoom control.
- **Embedded Samples & Reactions (`Add Sample` / `Add Reaction`)**: Drag and drop existing [samples](samples) or [reactions](reactions) from the element panel to cross-reference experimental records.

### Organizing segments

- Hover over a segment header and drag the handle <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faArrowsAlt}/> to reorder segments.
- Unwanted segments can be deleted from the segment controls.

## Edit mode, view mode, and tabs

Research plans support an **Edit mode** for adding, editing, and reordering segments, and a **View mode** displaying a formatted preview. In addition to the main plan content, the details panel provides **Analyses**, **Attachments**, **References**, **Wellplates**, **Metadata**, and **History** tabs, and supports scheduling via the [Calendar](../calendar).

## Exporting research plans

From **View mode**, saved research plans can be exported to:
- **Microsoft Word (`.docx`)**
- **OpenDocument Text (`.odt`)**
- **HTML** (`.zip` archive)
- **Markdown** (`.zip` archive)
- **LaTeX** (`.zip` archive)

Export is disabled while the research plan has unsaved edits; save changes first before exporting.

## Searching research plans

Within the currently selected collection (or personal **All** view), research plans can be searched via **Advanced Search** across **Name**, **Text**, **Table**, and **Private Note** fields, as well as indirectly via linked samples, reactions, wellplates, or literature.

The main way to enter chemical structures into the Chemotion ELN is the Ketcher Editor. The editor is available via the [sample](/docs/eln/ui/elements/samples) detail modal and can be opened by clicking on the area framed in light blue. The Ketcher Editor is not a product of the Chemotion programming group, but it has been modified so that it can be used flexibly for a wide variety of applications. After the additions are made, the structure editor integrated within the Chemotion ELN is now unique in its functionality and, in addition to the input of small chemical molecules, it also enables the illustration of polymeric carrier materials (beads). The editor will also be supplemented by further developments in the future.

:::note[New in version 3]

From Chemotion ELN version 3 onwards, [EPAM Ketcher](https://lifescience.opensource.epam.com/ketcher/) is the default structure editor. It has to be enabled as a feature from the [administration interface](/docs/eln/admin) — see [Configuration](/docs/eln/install-configure/configuration).

:::

The Ketcher Structure Editor offers all the basic functions necessary for chemical work: drawing atoms and bonds, selecting and editing structures, rotating and flipping, applying S-groups and R-group labels, and computing molecular properties. Drawn molecules can be rotated, optimized, copied, and pasted. The view can be adjusted with the zoom control.

Several tools have a small black arrow in the lower right corner of their icon — this indicates a sub-menu with related tool variants (for example, different bond types or selection modes).

## Left Toolbar

The left toolbar runs vertically along the left edge of the canvas and contains all drawing and editing tools.

### Hand Tool

The **Hand Tool** <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/hand_tool.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Hand Tool"}/> (`Ctrl+Alt+H`) lets users pan the canvas by clicking and dragging. It does not interact with atoms or bonds — it simply moves the view. Use it to navigate around a large structure without accidentally selecting or drawing anything.

### Select

The selection tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/select.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Select"} /> is a group of three tools that can be switched by clicking the icon:

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/select_tools.png").default}
  alt={"Selection tools sub-menu"}
/>

- **Rectangle Selection** — drag a rectangle to select everything inside it
- **Lasso Selection** — draw a free-form shape to select enclosed atoms and bonds
- **Fragment Selection** — click any atom to select the entire connected fragment it belongs to (all bonded atoms and bonds, but not disconnected elements such as reaction arrows)

The icon always shows the last-used selection mode.

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/atoms_properties.png").default}
  alt={"Atom Properties dialog"}
/>

Double-clicking an atom with any selection tool opens the **Atom Properties** dialog. Here users can modify: **Atom Type**, **Label**, **Number**, **Alias**, **Charge**, **Isotope (atomic mass)**, **Valence**, **Radical**, and query-specific attributes. Click **Apply** to confirm the changes.

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/bond_properties.png").default}
  alt={"Bond Properties dialog"}
/>

Double-clicking a bond opens the **Bond Properties** dialog, where users can set the bond **Type**, **Topology**, **Reacting Center**, and a **Custom Query**. Click **Apply** to confirm.

Many tools require a selection before they can act — for example **Copy** and **Cut**. Some tools like **Erase** also work directly on clicked elements without a prior selection.

### Erase

The **Erase** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/erase.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Erase"}/> removes atoms, bonds, or other elements from the canvas. Click directly on any element to erase it, or drag a rectangle over multiple elements to erase them all at once. Alternatively, select elements first with the selection tool and press **Delete** or **Backspace**.

When an atom is erased, all its bonds are erased too. When a bond is erased, the attached atoms remain unless they have no other bonds.

### Rotate and Flip

In EPAM Ketcher, rotation and flip controls appear contextually — they are not fixed toolbar buttons.

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/rotate_context.png").default}
  alt={"Selected molecule showing rotation handle and floating flip buttons"}
/>

**Rotating:** Select a structure to reveal a blue rotation handle above the selection box. Click and drag the handle to rotate the selection freely around its centre.

**Flipping:** When a structure is selected, two floating action buttons appear above it:

- <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/horizontal.png").default} width={"32px"} alt={"Horizontal Flip"}/> **Horizontal Flip** (`Alt+H`) — mirrors the selection left–right (flips around the vertical axis)
- <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/vertical.png").default} width={"32px"} alt={"Vertical Flip"}/> **Vertical Flip** (`Alt+V`) — mirrors the selection top–bottom (flips around the horizontal axis)

Both controls require a selection to be active. Use any of the selection tools first (see [Select](#select)).

### Bonds

![Bonds](/img/bondslist.png)

The bonds menu can be usually opened by using this icon <img src={require('@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/bond_single.png').default} width={"20px"} alt={"Single"}/> from Ketcher's left column. However, if another type of bonds is selected, the main icon changes to that type. Alongside the usual **Single**, **Double**, **Triple** bonds, Ketcher editor offers a variety of bonds to express coordination, stereochemistry, and aromaticity. All bond types can be seen in the image above. If a user draws a separate bond (not starting from an atom), it will be assumed to be between two carbon atoms, so with a **Single** bond the user gets CH3-CH3. If it starts from an atom, the end is assumed to be a carbon atom. However those carbon atoms can be replaced with the one the user chooses from the atoms column. A bond can also be used to connect two atoms or to change the type of an already existing bond to another one. If the bond is asymmetric, e.g. a stereochemical bond, the user can change its direction by re-clicking on the bond. Make sure that the right bond type is selected, otherwise the bond type changes instead of its direction.

![Ketcher bonds](/img/eln/ui/ketcher/ketcher_bonds.gif)

### Chain Tool

This tool <img src={require('@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/chain_tool.png').default} width={"20px"} alt={"Chain"} /> can be used to build chains of carbon (methylene groups) instead of taking time to add the groups one by one. If atoms other than carbon or bonds other than **Single** are wanted, one by one modifications are possible subsequently.

### Stereochemistry

:::note[Not available in v3.12]
The Stereochemistry tool is currently disabled in Chemotion ELN v3.12 and appears greyed out in the toolbar.
:::

The **Stereochemistry** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/stereochemistry.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Stereochemistry"}/> (`Alt+E`) applies enhanced stereochemistry annotations to stereocenters in the structure. After selecting the relevant atoms, the tool opens a dialog where users can assign stereo groups — **And**, **Or**, or **Absolute** — to indicate relative or absolute configuration. This is used to represent mixtures of enantiomers or diastereomers with precision beyond simple wedge bonds.

### Charge

![Change Atom Charge](/img/change_atom_charge.gif)

The user can modify the charge of an atom in one of two ways, both shown in the video above:

- Using the icons: increase the positivity with the icon <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/charge_plus.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"ChargeP"} />, or increase the negativity with the icon <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/charge_minus.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"ChargeM"} />. The user has to click on the desired icon and then click on the desired atom.
- Using text: type the charge directly next to the atom, e.g. `C+` or `N-`.

Either way, the change in charge is accompanied with change in the number of attached hydrogen atoms.

### S-Group

The **S-Group** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/sgroup.png").default} width={"30px"} alt={"S-Group"}/> (`Ctrl+G`) marks a selected set of atoms as an S-group (special group), used to annotate parts of a structure with chemical or query meaning.

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/sgroup_dialog.png").default}
  alt={"S-Group Properties dialog"}
/>

To use it: select the atoms you want to group, then press `Ctrl+G` or activate the tool and drag a rectangle around them. The **S-Group Properties** dialog opens with the following options:

- **Type** — the kind of S-group: **Data**, **Multiple Group**, **SRU Polymer**, **Superatom**, or **Query Component**
- **Context** — the scope: **Fragment**, **Multifragment**, or **Bond** (availability depends on Type)
- **Field name / Field value** — for **Data** S-groups, attach a named data field (e.g. a yield, a structure name, or a custom annotation) to the selected atoms
- **Absolute / Relative / Attached** — controls how the data label is positioned relative to the group

Click **Apply** to confirm. The S-group bracket appears around the selected atoms on the canvas.

### R-Group Label

The **R-Group Label** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/rgroup_label.png").default} width={"25px"} alt={"R-Group Label"}/> (`Ctrl+R`) assigns an R-group placeholder (R1–R32) to an atom, marking it as a variable attachment point in an R-group query structure.

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/rgroup_dialog.png").default}
  alt={"R-Group dialog showing R1 through R32"}
/>

Click the tool, then click an atom on the canvas. The **R-Group** dialog opens showing R1–R32. Select the desired label and click **Apply**. The atom is replaced by the chosen R-group placeholder. R-group structures are used in substructure search and combinatorial chemistry workflows.

### Monomer (Bead)

:::note[Not available in v3.12]
The Monomer tool is not available in Chemotion ELN v3.12. Use the **Solid Surface Templates** in the top toolbar to place solid-support structures.
:::

For solid-phase syntheses, the **Create a Monomer** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/polymer.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Monomer"} /> places a bead symbol on the canvas. Similar to the bead symbol in ChemDraw, it represents the polymeric carrier material of a solid support. The ELN recognises this symbol and adjusts the sample input module layout accordingly. The load of the solid support is defined within the sample input model.

### Reaction Tools

Ketcher provides three tools for drawing reaction schemes:

- <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/reaction_plus.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Reaction Plus"}/> **Reaction Plus** — places a plus sign (`+`) between two structures to indicate multiple reactants or products in a single reaction scheme.
- <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/arrow_tool.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Arrow"}/> **Arrow Open Angle** — draws a reaction arrow between the reactant and product sides of the scheme. Several arrow styles are available via the sub-menu (small triangle in the corner of the icon).
- <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/reaction_mapping.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Mapping"}/> **Reaction Mapping Tool** — assigns atom-map numbers to atoms, correlating specific atoms in the reactants with the corresponding atoms in the products. Click an atom in the reactant, then click the corresponding atom in the product to create a mapping pair.

### Shape Ellipse

The **Shape Ellipse** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/shape_ellipse.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Shape Ellipse"}/> draws an ellipse on the canvas as a visual annotation. Click and drag to size the ellipse. Additional shape tools (rectangle, line) are available in the sub-menu. Shapes do not represent chemical structure — they are used solely for visual markup and highlighting regions of interest.

### Add Text

The **Add Text** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/add_text.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Add Text"}/> (`Alt+T`) places a text label anywhere on the canvas. Click the desired position to open a text input, type the label, then confirm. Text annotations are useful for adding notes, conditions, yield values, or headings to a reaction scheme. The text is part of the canvas and will be included when the structure is saved or exported.

### Add Image

The **Add Image** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/add_image.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Add Image"}/> inserts an image file onto the canvas. Click to open the file picker, select an image (PNG, JPG, SVG), and it will be placed on the canvas. Like text annotations, embedded images are saved and exported with the structure.

## Top Toolbar

The top toolbar runs horizontally across the top of the canvas and contains file operations, edit history, clipboard actions, zoom, and structure analysis tools.

### Clear Canvas

The tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/clear_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Clear"} /> deletes everything on the current canvas. This action can be undone with **Undo**.

### Open

The tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/open_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Open"} /> opens the **Open Structure** dialog. Structures can be loaded by:

- **Pasting text** — paste a Molfile, SMILES, InChI, or other supported format directly into the text area
- **Uploading a file** — use the file picker to browse for a structure file (`.mol`, `.sdf`, `.rxn`, etc.)

The structure can optionally be loaded as a fragment to be copied to the clipboard rather than placed directly on the canvas.

### Save As

The tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/save_as_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Save As"}/> opens the **Save Structure** dialog. The current canvas content can be exported in one of several formats, including:

- **MDL Molfile V2000 / V3000**
- **Daylight SMILES** and **Extended SMILES**
- **InChI** and **InChIKey**
- **CML**, **SDF**, and **RXN**

Structures can also be saved directly as a **custom template** by selecting **Template** from the format dropdown. Saved templates become available in the **Structure Library** (bottom toolbar → Templates Library), making them reusable across sessions.

Select the desired format from the dropdown, then copy the generated text or download it as a file.

### Undo/Redo

Any action in the editor can be reversed with **Undo** <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/undo_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Undo"}/> (`Ctrl+Z`), and a reversed action can be reapplied with **Redo** <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/redo_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Redo"}/> (`Ctrl+Y` / `Ctrl+Shift+Z`). Multiple undo/redo steps are supported.

### Cut/Copy/Paste

A selected structure or substructure can be cut <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/cut_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Cut"}/> (`Ctrl+X`), copied <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/copy_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Copy"}/> (`Ctrl+C`), and pasted <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/paste_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Paste"}/> (`Ctrl+V`) elsewhere on the canvas. A selection must be active before cutting or copying.

### Zoom

The zoom level is controlled via the percentage indicator in the top toolbar. Click it to open a dropdown with preset zoom levels, or use the keyboard shortcuts `Ctrl++` (zoom in) and `Ctrl+-` (zoom out). `Ctrl+0` resets the zoom to fit the structure on screen.

### Clean Up

The **Clean Up** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/clean_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Clean Up"}/> automatically improves the layout of the structure on the canvas by normalising bond lengths, angles, and atom spacing. It can be applied to the whole canvas or to a selection only.

### Calculated Values

The **Calculated Values** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/show_molecule_info_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Calculated Values"}/> opens a dialog that displays computed properties for the structure on the canvas:

<img
  src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/molecule_info.png").default}
  alt={"Calculated Values dialog"}
/>

Properties shown include **Molecular Weight**, **Exact Molecular Weight**, **Molecular Formula**, **Elemental Analysis**, and **Largest Fragment** details. If elements are selected, the values reflect the selection only; otherwise the entire canvas is used.

### Aromatize / Dearomatize

The **Aromatize** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/aromatize_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Aromatize"}/> (`Alt+A`) converts alternating single and double bonds in a ring to an aromatic representation (Kekulé → aromatic). The **Dearomatize** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/dearomatize_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Dearomatize"}/> (`Ctrl+Alt+A`) does the reverse, assigning explicit alternating bonds. Both tools operate on the entire canvas or on a selection.

### Layout

The **Layout** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/layout_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Layout"}/> (`Ctrl+L`) recalculates the 2D coordinates of the entire structure using an automatic layout algorithm, spreading atoms evenly and untangling overlapping bonds. Use it when a pasted or imported structure has a poor initial layout.

### Calculate CIP

The **Calculate CIP** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/calculate_cip_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Calculate CIP"}/> (`Ctrl+P`) assigns Cahn–Ingold–Prelog (CIP) stereo descriptors (R/S for stereocentres, E/Z for double bonds) and displays them directly on the canvas next to the relevant atoms or bonds.

### Check Structure

The **Check Structure** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/check_structure_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Check Structure"}/> (`Alt+S`) runs a set of structural validation checks on the current canvas and reports any issues — such as valence errors, overlapping atoms, or ambiguous stereo bonds — in a dialog. Use it to catch drawing errors before saving.

### Add/Remove Explicit Hydrogens

The **Add/Remove explicit hydrogens** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/explicit_hydrogens_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Explicit Hydrogens"}/> toggles between showing and hiding explicit hydrogen atoms on the canvas. When hydrogens are added explicitly, each hydrogen appears as a labelled atom with a bond. When removed, they revert to the implicit representation.

### 3D Viewer

The **3D Viewer** <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/3d_viewer_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"3D Viewer"}/> opens a three-dimensional view of the current structure in a separate dialog, generated by converting the 2D drawing to a 3D conformation. This is useful for a quick spatial sanity-check of a structure.

### Solid Surface Templates

The **Solid Surface Templates** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/solid_surface_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Solid Surface Templates"}/> opens a library of pre-defined solid-support and bead templates. Select a template and place it on the canvas to represent a polymeric carrier in a solid-phase synthesis scheme.

The dialog has **Basic** and **Semantic** tabs and organizes templates into three top-level categories:

- **Active Phase (Round)** — round bead supports, further broken down into Single-compound active phase, Multi-compound active phase, and Promotors
- **Support (Rectangle)** — flat rectangular solid-support representations
- **Body (Rectangle)** — inert rectangular carrier bodies

Click a category to expand it, then click a thumbnail to insert the corresponding bead or support onto the canvas. The placed structure includes attachment points where you can continue drawing the chemistry anchored to the solid support.

![Solid Surface Chemistry Templates in action](/img/eln/ui/ketcher/solid_surface_chemistry.gif)

### Add Label

The **Add Label** tool <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/add_label_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Add Label"}/> inserts a predefined abbreviation or superatom label at a selected position. This provides a shorthand notation for common functional groups (e.g. Boc, Ts, Me) without drawing every atom individually.

### Settings

The **Settings** button <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/settings_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Settings"}/> opens the Ketcher settings dialog, where users can adjust display options such as atom numbering, bond length, stereochemistry display style, and font size.

### Fullscreen

The **Fullscreen** button <img src={require("@site/static/img/eln/ui/ketcher/fullscreen_icon.png").default} width={"20px"} alt={"Fullscreen"}/> expands the Ketcher editor to fill the entire browser window. Press it again (or use `Escape`) to return to the embedded view. Fullscreen mode is especially useful when drawing complex structures that need more canvas space.

## Right Toolbar

The right toolbar runs vertically along the right edge of the canvas and provides quick access to the most commonly used atom types, as well as the Periodic Table and generic atom groups.

### Atoms

![Ketcher atoms](/img/atoms.gif)

The most frequently used atoms are displayed directly as clickable symbols: **H**, **C**, **N**, **O**, **S**, **P**, **F**, **Cl**, **Br**, **I**. Click an atom symbol to activate it, then click anywhere on the canvas to place it, or click an existing atom to replace it.

Less common elements can be accessed via the <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faEllipsisH} size="lg"/> icon, which opens the **Periodic Table**. From there the user can select a **Single** atom, define a **List** (any atom from the list matches in a query), or a **Not List** (any atom not in the list). **Generic Groups** (such as Reaxys Generics) are also available for query structures.

When an atom is placed on the canvas, it is assigned hydrogen atoms automatically based on its valency. For example, placing carbon **C** gives CH₄. The hydrogen count adjusts dynamically as bonds are added or the charge is changed. The valency and explicit hydrogen count can be overridden via the **Atom Properties** dialog (see [Select](#select)).

## Bottom Toolbar

The bottom toolbar runs horizontally along the bottom of the left column and provides quick access to ring templates and the built-in structure library.

### Rings

![Ring templates](/img/rings.png)

The rings toolbar offers quick access to common ring templates: **benzene** (aromatic), **cyclopentadiene** (5-membered, one double bond), **cyclohexane** (6), **cyclopentane** (5), **cyclopropane** (3), **cyclobutane** (4), **cycloheptane** (7), and **cyclooctane** (8). All rings contain carbon atoms only, but atoms and bonds can be modified afterwards using the atoms column or bond tools. If a larger ring is needed, open the closest available ring, add new atoms, and close it again with a bond.

### Structure Library

The toolbar ends with the ![Structure Library](/img/icon_structurelibrary.svg) icon, which opens the **Structure Library** built into Ketcher — a searchable collection of pre-defined structures organised into three categories:

- **Templates Library** — common chemical scaffolds and ring systems for quick insertion
- **Functional Groups** — frequently used functional group fragments
- **Salts and Solvents** — standard salt counter-ions and common solvent molecules

Click a structure in the library to select it, then click on the canvas to place it.

![Structure Library UI](/img/structuredlibraryui.png)

If a structure is missing from the library, contact us to request a new template.

## Standard templates

The specified standard templates are given and cannot be changed. Users who have suggestions or guidelines for additional templates or even a new template category can send a MolFile or a ChemDraw file to nicole.jung@kit.edu. The Chemotion team will review the structure and add it to the standard templates.
The standard templates can be found in the lower bar of the editor and are arranged according to the categories Amino Acids, Aromatics, Bicyclics, Conformers, Cycloalkanes, Functional Groups, Nucleic Acids, Paracyclophanes, Schlegel-diagrams and Solid Supports. Press on a category to open its template list, press again to close it.

:::danger[Caution]

When creating chiral compounds in Chemotion ELN, the configuration of the unique structure is observed and registered accordingly. Planar chiral compounds are unfortunately not recognized as such yet, and therefore, they are not differentiated in lists and names, even if they were drawn accordingly by the Ketcher editor. An example is the Paracyclophanes. Please note the chirality of the samples in those cases in the **Description** field.

This limitation applies to the Chemotion ELN system and is not specific to the Ketcher editor.

:::

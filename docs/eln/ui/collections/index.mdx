## Collections: Organization, Function and Usage

A **collection** groups the elements you work with — samples, reactions, wellplates,
screens, research plans and others — usually along the lines of a project or a
sub-project. Collections are the backbone of the ELN: without at least one of your own,
there is nowhere to put your work.

### The collection bar

The collection bar sits on the left of the main window and is used to navigate between
collections. Some of its symbols:

| symbol | meaning |
| ------ | ------- |
| <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faList} size="sm"/> | toggle the collection bar on/off |
| <Btn mixed={[faCog]} color={"danger"}/> | open collection management |
| <Btn mixed={[faPlus]} color={"success"}/> | open sub-collections |
| <Btn mixed={[faMinus]} color={"success"}/> | close sub-collections |
| <Btn mixed={[faShareAlt]} color={"secondary"}/> | this collection is shared — hover it to see with whom, or who shared it with you |

![The collection bar with its three groups](/img/collections/tree_overview.png)

The bar is organized into up to three groups:

- **My Collections** — the collections you own. Selecting the group header itself opens
  the [**All**](#system-collections) collection.
- **Shared with me** — collections other people or groups have shared with you, listed
  under a row naming each owner. See [Sharing Collections](./sharing.mdx#shared-with-me).
- **chemotion-repo** — the repository collections, described
  [below](#system-collections).

:::info[There is no separate branch for collections you share out]

A collection that you own and have shared with someone else stays where it is, in **My
Collections**. It is marked with the share icon
<Btn mixed={[faShareAlt]} color={"secondary"}/>, and hovering that icon lists the people
and groups you shared it with, together with what each of them may do.

:::

### System collections

Some collections are provided by the ELN itself, because each has a job to do. They are
**locked**: their name, position and place in the tree are fixed, and they can never be
renamed, moved, deleted, or nested inside another collection.

Two of them, **All** and **chemotion-repository.net**, exist from the moment your account
is created. The third, **transferred**, appears inside the repository subtree the first
time you hand data over to the repository.

#### `All`

**All** gathers every element you own, across all of your collections. It is a safety net:
if an element ever loses its assignment to a working collection, it is still reachable
here and can be assigned again.

It is also the one place that searches across everything you own — useful when you are not
sure which collection an entry belongs to.

:::caution

**All** is a backup view, not a workspace. While you are in **All**, moving elements and
*Remove from current Collection* are disabled — there is no "current collection" to move
them out of. Switch to one of your working collections to reorganize your elements.

:::

#### `chemotion-repository.net`

The **[chemotion-repository.net](https://www.chemotion-repository.net/home/welcome)**
collection is the staging area for publishing to the Chemotion repository.

On its own it is just a folder for structuring your own work — nothing in it is visible
from outside. When you are ready to publish, you select the data in this collection and
confirm their release to the repository. If the release falls within the term of a
competition (announced regularly on
[chemotion-repository.net](https://www.chemotion-repository.net/home/welcome)), the
entries are registered for it.

#### `transferred`

**transferred** holds the elements that have already been handed over to the repository. It
sits inside the repository subtree and is created for you the first time data is
transferred, so you will not see it before then.

#### Working with system collections

Although they cannot be restructured, system collections are not read-only. In collection
management they appear in a pinned **System collections** section above your editable
tree, where you can:

- **Add share** and **Manage shares** — share them like any other collection, and
- **Edit collection tabs** — set the element tabs shown for them.

Two particularities are worth knowing:

- Sharing **All** hands the recipient access to *every element you own*, including
  elements you add later, so the ELN asks you to confirm first.
- *Edit collection tabs* on **All** edits your **default** tab layout — the one used
  wherever no collection-specific layout applies.

![System collections in collection management](/img/collections/management_system.png)

Ownership of a system collection can never be passed on.

### Sorting and organizing

Collections are created and organized in **collection management**, opened with the
<Btn mixed={[faCog]} color={"danger"}/> button in the collection bar. Press
<Btn mixed={[faPlus]} color={"success"}/> to add a **New Collection** under **My
Collections**, give it a name, and save it with
<Btn mixed={["Update"]} color={"warning"}/>.

:::caution

Remember to press <Btn mixed={["Update"]} color={"warning"}/>. Without that confirmation,
no collection is created and no reordering is kept.

:::

Think about a meaningful structure before you start. You can create as many collections as
you like, but a journal you can still find your way around matters more. As a rule it
makes sense to begin with one collection as your electronic journal, and perhaps a second
one reproducing your chemical database.

Each collection can be organized into sub-collections, which in turn can contain further
sub-collections — much like folders on your computer. A sub-collection is created the same
way as a collection, and becomes a sub-collection by being indented to the right, by hand
or by drag and drop. Again, the change takes effect only after
<Btn mixed={["Update"]} color={"warning"}/>.

:::info

Sub-collections structure your work, but their contents are **not** mirrored into the
collection above them. Keep this in mind when acting on a collection as a whole — when
sharing it, for instance, where a separate option exists to include the whole branch.

:::

Collections you own can be sorted and restructured at any time. Collections that were
shared with you cannot: they belong to their owner, and only the owner can rename, move or
delete them.

### Visibility

Your own collections are always visible in the collection bar. Collections you have shared
out are marked with <Btn mixed={[faShareAlt]} color={"secondary"}/> so that it stays
obvious which of your data other people can reach, and collections shared with you appear
separately under **Shared with me**, grouped by their owner.

![Shared collections marked in the collection bar](/img/collections/tree.png)

How to share a collection, what each permission level allows, and what recipients can and
cannot do, is described in [Sharing Collections](./sharing.mdx).

:::info["My projects with others"]

After an upgrade from version 3, you may find a collection named **My projects with
others**. Earlier versions listed collections you had shared in a separate branch of the
tree; version 4 does not, so the upgrade gathered those collections under this one node to
keep them together.

It is an ordinary collection — you can rename it, move its contents, or delete it once you
have reorganized things to your liking.

:::

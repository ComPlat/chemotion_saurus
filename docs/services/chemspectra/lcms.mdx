title: LC/MS
---

:::info[Supported file formats]

For LC/MS analysis, the Chemotion team currently supports the
[JCAMP-DX file](https://iupac.org/what-we-do/digital-standards/jcamp-dx/) sets
produced by the converter from Agilent OpenLab and ChemStation data, holding
the UV/VIS, TIC and mass traces together.

:::

LC/MS analysis is available from Chemotion ELN version 4.0.

1. To upload and view their data, users can follow
   [this instruction](../chemspectra#how-to-use-chemspectra). An analysis whose
   dataset holds an LC/MS set opens in the LC/MS layout.

2. The editor shows three charts, one above the other: the UV/VIS
   chromatogram, the total ion current (TIC), and the mass spectrum.

3. **Clicking the TIC chart chooses a retention time.** A red marker is drawn
   where the click landed, and the mass spectrum for that moment is fetched and
   drawn below. Nothing is precomputed, so a spectrum appears a moment after
   the click rather than instantly.

4. Where the data hold more than one polarity, a select labelled **TIC** offers
   **PLUS**, **MINUS** and **NEUTRAL**, and the mass spectrum is limited to the
   one chosen. A second select picks the **Wavelength (nm)** for the UV/VIS
   trace.

5. The retention time and polarity last used are remembered while the browser
   session lasts, so reopening the analysis returns to the same view.

6. Saving offers the usual choices — **save**, **save & close**, **write peak &
   save** and **write peak, save & close**.

:::note

The curve dropdown in the window header is inactive in this layout: the chart
to work on is chosen by clicking it.

:::

The **Visualization Tab** in OpenStats allows users to explore and visualize data
through several plot types, including *Boxplots*, *Scatterplots*, and *Lineplots*.  
The most important features are described in detail below.

## Basic Plot

The example below shows how to plot the CO<sub>2</sub> uptake of plants against the CO<sub>2</sub> concentration.

## Investigate the treatment effect

import FillSideBar from '@site/static/img/services/third_party_apps/OpenStats/visualization/FillSideBar.png';
import FillPlot from '@site/static/img/services/third_party_apps/OpenStats/visualization/FillPlot.png';

Plants are measured at different CO<sub>2</sub> concentrations and at two treatments called
*chilled* and *non-chilled*.
To visualize the effect of the treatments, the fill color of the Boxplots can be set based on
the treatment group. As shown below, for each concentration one boxplot for *chilled* plants (light blue)
and one for *non-chilled* plants (green) are shown.

## Investigate the location effect

Moreover, plants are measured at two different locations, Mississippi and Quebec.
To check whether the location has an effect on the CO<sub>2</sub> uptake, the original
plot can be split into sub-plots. To do this, the column containing the location information
(called *Type*) has to be chosen in a drop-down menu.

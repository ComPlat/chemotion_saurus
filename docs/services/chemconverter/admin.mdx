## Enable/Disable users as ChemConverter Administrator

To assign the ChemConverter Administrator role, navigate to the [User Management](/docs/eln/admin/user-management) menu, select the desired user, and click on **Enable Converter profiles editing for this user** <FontAwesomeIcon icon={faHourglassHalf} />.
If the button is green, it indicates that the user has Converter management permission; otherwise, they do not have the permission.

<div align="center">
  <img src={permissionManagement} alt="UI Feature" width="800" />
</div>

When the administrator clicks on the **Enable Converter profiles editing for this user** button, a message confirms the granting of converter management permission to this user, displayed as _'Enable Converter profiles editing for this user'_.
Likewise, when the administrator grants converter management permission to this user, a message confirms the disabling of converter profiles editing, displayed as _'Disable Converter profiles editing for this user'_.

:::info[Permissions]

The ChemConverter Administrator permission is granted by the system administrator.

:::

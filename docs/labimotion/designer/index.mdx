Designers can work with a variety of component types, ranging from simple text fields to tables. This empowers them to construct intricate workflows tailored to their requirements.

As a designer, one can take advantage of the following capabilities:

- Easily synchronize templates suitable for the application from the Template Hub. This approach allows designers to benefit from the collaborative efforts of scientists and is **highly recommended**.
- A wide variety of **[Components](designer/components/)**, allowing designers to approach their design methodically, step by step.

:::info[Permissions]

The Designer role encompasses [Element](/docs/labimotion/designer/elements), [Segment](/docs/labimotion/designer/segments), and [Dataset](/docs/labimotion/designer/datasets) functions, which are accessible to individuals with designer permissions. If the **Generic Designer** option cannot be located in the menu, reach out to the system administrator for assistance.

<img src={menu} alt="Menu" />

:::

## Recommended practices

### Sync templates from Template Hub

Utilize the Template Hub, a user-friendly collaboration center designed for scientists to effortlessly share templates across different applications. Synchronizing templates through the Template Hub is a streamlined process.

Watch the video below to see how a designer can efficiently sync templates to their own instance.

## Getting Started

1. Select [**Generic Element Designer**](./elements/index.mdx), [**Generic Segment Designer**](./segments/index.mdx), or [**Generic Dataset Designer**](./datasets/index.mdx) from the menu.

<img src={designerMenu} alt="Designer Menu" />

2. Locate the desired template and click the [**Template**](./template-features/index.mdx) button - the system will automatically navigate to the [**Work Area**](./template-features/workarea.mdx).

Starting with LabIMotion v2, designers can use **Quick Filter** to quickly find a template by entering a piece of text.

<YouTubeFrame
  src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/uYP60ApVTsI"
  title="Designer, you design what you need."
/>

<FigCaption text="LabIMotion - Quick Filter" />


3. Utilize the [**Preview Design**](./template-features/preview-design.mdx) feature to gain a visual representation of the template's appearance.
4. Access the [**Work Area**](./template-features/workarea.mdx) to integrate essential components, arrange them as preferred, and establish the desired layout.
